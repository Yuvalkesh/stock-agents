## Key Lessons Learned

### 1. **Over-Filtering Destroys Edge**
The learning log explicitly flags this:
> *"LESSON: This filter was too strict."* (XOM, CVX, CRWD, FTNT entries)

Agent 4's risk filter is **eliminating valid, profitable setups.** This violates fundamental trading logic: **avoiding small wins is equivalent to accepting small losses in expected value.**

### 2. **Strategy-Specific Thresholds Needed**
Not all strategies should share the same Agent 4 score threshold:
- **MACD+RSI:** More momentum-driven → may require lower threshold
- **MA Crossover:** Trend-based → may tolerate higher false-positive rate than current filter allows
- **Connors RSI:** Mean-reversion specific → different risk profile entirely

### 3. **Macro Filter (Agent 1) Functioned Correctly**
- MIXED regime correctly identified on 9/18 and 9/23
- This is appropriate conservatism during transition periods
- But does NOT explain why setups were missed *during* valid trading windows

### 4. **Target Hits Validate Strategy Logic**
The fact that 6 missed trades hit targets within 2–5 days proves:
- Entry signals were technically sound
- The underlying strategies ARE working
- The problem is **filtering**, not strategy selection
