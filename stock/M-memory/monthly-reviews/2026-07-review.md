#### 2. **Implement Weighted Scoring** (MEDIUM PRIORITY)
- **Current:** Sequential veto gates (if Agent 4 says NO, signal dies)
- **Proposed:** Composite score = 40% MA signal + 30% Gatekeeper + 20% Agent 4 + 10% Macro
- **Benefit:** Allows "partially qualified" signals through instead of binary accept/reject

- **Observation:** Winning targets averaged +1.5% (very tight)
